Reflection on Jury #1 Feedback

What did you decide to change, refine, or revise based on jury feedback?
I decided to create a body for the design to give it a tangible form and help the next jury better understand what my system can do visually and functionally. I also added more LEDs and a potentiometer for greater control and responsiveness.

What materials (presentation, written documentation, or prototype) need the most improvement?
I plan to extend the LED setup from 5 to 25 addressable LEDs, add three additional potentiometers for parameter control, and ideally 3D-print a custom housing to encase the system and enhance presentation quality.

Retrospective (After Jury #1)

What went well:
The presentation successfully demonstrated the system’s core features and concept.

What didn’t:
The lack of a defined body or enclosure made it harder for the jury to visualize the final product’s real-world application.

What I learned:
Form and presentation are equally important as function. Giving the prototype a physical identity helps communicate design intent and use case more effectively.
